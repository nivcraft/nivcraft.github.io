What you can do

Everything is simulated. NivCraft is a practice and research tool, not a brokerage.

Live watchlists

Track equities, indices, futures, options and crypto across list and grid views, with live quotes and colour-coded moves.

Simulated trading

Open and close positions with virtual funds. Track P&L, margin, and a full trade history over time.

Backtesting

Run a strategy over historical data and see how it would have performed, including open-position mark-to-market.

Signals & alerts

Live F&O scan

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google — your account keeps your watchlists and history.
  2. Connect an Indian broker (optional) for live NSE prices — crypto works out of the box.
  3. Build a watchlist of the instruments you want to follow.
  4. Place simulated trades and track how your ideas perform over time.

FAQ

Is this real trading?

No. Every trade in NivCraft is simulated with virtual funds. NivCraft is not a broker, does not execute real orders, and nothing in the app is investment advice.

Do I need a broker account?

It's optional. Crypto markets work with no account at all. Connecting an Indian brokerage account (FYERS or 5paisa) adds live NSE equity and F&O data to your watchlists and simulations.

Is my data private?

Yes. Live Indian market data is retrieved using your own broker credentials on your device and is never shared with other users. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What does it cost?

NivCraft is free with ads, with an optional premium tier that removes ads and raises usage limits.

When does it launch?
